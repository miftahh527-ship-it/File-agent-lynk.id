## English version (for Griffith University)

**Subject:** Permission request — Liang Metanduno hand stencil photo for a children's educational video

Dear Griffith University Media Team / research team,

My name is Miftah Hadi and I run **Let.growww**, an Indonesian educational YouTube channel for children aged 9–14. We are producing an animated video, *"300,000 Years: From Caves to Smartphones"*, which features the hand stencil at Liang Metanduno, Muna Island — the oldest dated cave art in the world (at least 67,800 years old), as published in *Nature* in January 2026.

We would like to request permission to show **one or two photographs of the hand stencil** (from the press release or the paper) in the video and its thumbnail. Each photo would be shown unaltered with a visible credit directly beneath it (e.g. "Photo: [photographer/institution]") and repeated in the video description. The video is educational; the channel may carry YouTube ads.

Please let us know the credit line you prefer and any conditions of use. Thank you for supporting science education for Indonesian children.
